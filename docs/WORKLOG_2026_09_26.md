## 5. New ladder from a real IMO problem

"Parity of Sum of Digits and Polynomial" is **IMO Shortlist 2022 A7** (official wording). Following the
ladder rules (use ideas, never wording), a new ladder was built from its idea: *far-apart numbers add
without carries, and each carry lowers a digit sum by exactly 9*.

**Ladder F — Carries Cost Nine** (s(n) = digit sum of n):

| Part | Level | Question | Answer |
|---|---|---|---|
| Step 1 | Kangaroo | s(999) + s(1) − s(1000) | 27 |
| Step 2 | Kangaroo / MATHCOUNTS | s(4768) + s(1325) − s(4768 + 1325) | 18 |
| Step 3 | MATHCOUNTS | s(A) = 50, s(B) = 40, exactly 6 carries: s(A + B)? | 36 |
| Step 4 | AMC 8 | s(1001 × 1001 × 1001) | 8 |
| Step 5 | AMC 10 | s(1001⁶) | 28 |
| Bridge | AMC 8 / MATHCOUNTS | Q(x) = x² + 9x: s(Q(10)) − s(Q(1)) | 9 |
| Challenge | AIME | sum of s(Q(101 × 10ᵐ)) for m = 0…20 | 435 |

Endings: related problems ISL 2022 A7, ISL 2016 N1, IMO 1975 P4; Explore = Kummer's theorem; open problem =
Erdős's powers of 2 in base 3 (status checked 2026-09-26). Written up in
`olympiad/ladders/LADDERS_v0.1.md`; answers checked in `olympiad/ladders/verify_ladders.py`
(now 55 checks, all pass). A web search found no existing problem with the Challenge's twist.
