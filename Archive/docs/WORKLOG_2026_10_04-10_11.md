## 3. New ladder structure (decided 2026-10-10)

Rules: [olympiad/ladders/LADDER_RULES_v2.md](../olympiad/ladders/LADDER_RULES_v2.md); policy: OPERATING_POLICY 6.5.

- A ladder is **the solution of one hard Challenge cut into moves**, not a staircase of contest levels:
  idea card(s) → new hard Challenge (never the original statement) → our full solution → **Problem analysis**
  → **Logic flow** → one short-answer step per move → links (similar olympiad problems, research, similar site
  ladders, proof problem).
- Every step is AMC 8 / Kangaroo / MATHCOUNTS level or higher.
- **Short answer only**: proof grading has no agreed criteria and costs AI fees.
- Analysis and flow are **members only** (served by signed-in API routes, never with the public key).
- A Challenge may combine several idea cards. English now; Korean later with the Korean competition data.

## 5. Short-answer grading (fixed 2026-10-11)

- The Short answer tab's Check button now works: `/api/check-answer` compares the number exactly (fractions,
  commas, spaces and `$…$` accepted) with the stored answer (first line of the solution, `Answer: …`).
- Signed in only; 20 checks per 10 minutes per user.
- XP only for the **first** correct solve; wrong short answers give 0 XP.
- The saving code was moved out of `grade-solution` into `src/lib/submissions.ts` so both routes share it
  (`grade-solution` behaves as before). Approved by Mookwon although it touches the grading area.
